Tile Coach — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-08

Tile Coach operates entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any user data. It has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no accounts, no camera access, and no billing integration.

The board, rack, and selected rule mode are saved in app-private preferences on your device only. Session undo history stays in memory and is cleared when the app closes. Cloud backup is disabled and saved preferences are excluded from device-to-device transfer. Use the in-app Clear action to reset the saved board/rack, or uninstall the app to remove all app data.

Tile Coach is an independent, unaffiliated practice tool. It is not associated with Hasbro, Mattel, Zynga, or any official Scrabble or Words With Friends product. The bundled ENABLE word list is a public-domain reference list, not an official tournament dictionary.

The operating system or app store may process data independently under their own separate policies.
