Daily Word — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-08

Daily Word operates entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any user data. It has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no accounts, and no billing integration.

The daily puzzle is generated deterministically on your device from the calendar date — no server, no network call. Your guess history, streak, and stats are stored in app-private storage on your device only. Cloud backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.

Daily Word is an independent, unaffiliated word game. It is not associated with the New York Times or any official Wordle product.

The operating system or app store may process data independently under their own separate policies.
