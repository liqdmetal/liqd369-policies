Anagram Wizard — Privacy Policy

Policy version: 1.1 · Effective: September 15, 2026 · Last updated: September 15, 2026

Data controller and contact

Developer/data controller: liqdmetal

For privacy questions or requests, email liqdmetal369@pm.me. Please use the subject “Privacy Request” when asking about personal information.

How Anagram Wizard handles data

Anagram Wizard operates entirely offline in the app. It does not collect, transmit, or share user data with liqdmetal or third parties. It has no internet permission, analytics, advertising SDK, accounts, or billing integration.

Your search history, saved word lists, and preferences are stored in app-private storage on your device only. No developer-controlled account or server-side sync is provided.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local search history, saved word lists, and preferences remain on your device until you use an in-app reset or clear control, where available, or uninstall the app. The developer has no server-side copy and cannot remotely erase data that exists only on your device.

The operating system or app store may process data independently under its own policies. Platform backups and device-to-device transfers are controlled by the operating system and may vary by platform version, device, and the released app configuration. The app does not access or control those platform-managed copies; check your platform settings and policies if you need to manage them.

If you contact us by email, we use the information in your message to respond to your request and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for support, security, and legal purposes.

Audience and third parties

Anagram Wizard is a general-audience word-game app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us at liqdmetal369@pm.me.

This policy applies to the app. The operating system, app store, and other platform providers may process data independently under their own policies.

This policy website

This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes under GitHub's Privacy Statement. That hosting activity is separate from Anagram Wizard: the app does not send game data to this website or to liqdmetal.

Word-list attribution

The bundled ENABLE word list is a public-domain reference list. Its documentation asks game designers to credit its originators; the source documentation is available in the ENABLE word-list reference.

Policy version 1.1. If app permissions, SDKs, storage, networking, backups, or audience change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures must be updated.
