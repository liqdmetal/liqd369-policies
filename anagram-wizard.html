Anagram Wizard — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-08

Anagram Wizard operates entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any user data. It has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no accounts, and no billing integration.

Your search history, saved word lists, and preferences are stored in app-private storage on your device only. Cloud backup is disabled and this data is excluded from device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.

The bundled ENABLE word list is a public-domain reference list.

The operating system or app store may process data independently under their own separate policies.
